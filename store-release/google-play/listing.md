# CarePass — Google Play listing

App name: CarePass

Short description:
Find healthcare providers and explore CarePass membership benefits in Ghana.

Full description:

CarePass helps you explore healthcare providers and membership benefits in Ghana, all in one place.

FIND CARE NEAR YOU
Browse hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, laboratories and diagnostic centres. Search for providers and services, explore nearby options and view contact details and directions.

EXPLORE MEMBERSHIP BENEFITS
View available CarePass plans and their benefits. Eligible members can access a digital membership card and manage supported family-member details in the app. Discounts and benefits depend on the selected plan and participating provider.

STAY INFORMED
Find answers to common questions, contact support and receive relevant account and membership notifications.

CarePass is a membership service, not health insurance. Provider availability, pricing, services and benefits may vary. Confirm details directly with the provider before visiting. Information in the app does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. For a medical emergency, contact local emergency services.
