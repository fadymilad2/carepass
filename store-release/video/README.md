# Android preview recording

`carepass-android-preview.mp4` is a silent 24.33-second screen recording of the release Android app, showing services, providers and provider details. Resolution is 1080 × 1920, encoded as H.264 at 30 fps. The emulator produces abrupt page transitions; review the video before publishing.

For Google Play, upload the MP4 to your own YouTube account as an embeddable public or unlisted video with monetization and age restriction disabled, then enter its URL in Play Console. This task did not upload or publish the video.

Do not use this Android recording as an Apple App Store preview. Capture the actual iOS app on a Mac/device using the handoff guide.

Source: [Google Play preview assets](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151).
